# Incoterms 2020

| Term | Seller pays up to | Risk passes |
|---|---|---|
| EXW | Goods ready at seller's premises | At seller's premises |
| FOB | Loaded on board at origin port | On board at origin |
| CIF | Freight and insurance to destination port | On board at origin |
| DAP | Delivery to buyer's address, duty unpaid | At destination |
| DDP | Everything, including import duty and VAT | At destination |

## Notes

- FOB and CIF are for sea freight only; for containers and air use FCA and CIP.
- Under DDP the seller must be able to act as importer of record in the destination country.
- CIF insurance only needs to be ICC (C) minimum cover unless agreed otherwise.
