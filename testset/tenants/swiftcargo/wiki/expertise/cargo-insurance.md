# Cargo insurance

## Institute Cargo Clauses

| Clause | Cover |
|---|---|
| ICC (A) | All risks, except exclusions such as poor packing, delay and inherent vice |
| ICC (B) | Named perils: fire, sinking, collision, earthquake, water entering the container |
| ICC (C) | Fewer named perils: fire, sinking, collision, jettison |

## Practice

- Sum insured: 110% of the CIF value.
- Carrier liability is limited (for example about 2 SDR per kg under the Hague-Visby rules), so
  high-value goods need their own insurance.
- Notice of claim within 7 days of delivery; full documents within 90 days.
