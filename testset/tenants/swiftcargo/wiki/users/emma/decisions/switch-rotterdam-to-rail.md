# Move 30% of Rotterdam volume to China-Europe rail

## Decision

Move 30% of Rotterdam-bound volume from the Shenzhen–Rotterdam sea lane (R-SZX-RTM) to the
Chongqing–Rotterdam rail lane (R-CKG-RTM) until congestion clears.

## Context

Rotterdam berthing delays reached 9 days and demurrage was piling up on time-sensitive auto parts.

## Outcome

Transit time for moved volume fell from 38 to 18 days; cost was about 1.6 times sea freight,
offset by avoided demurrage.
