# All-risks cover for high-value shipments

## Decision

Every shipment with a value above USD 50,000 is insured under ICC (A) at 110% of CIF value,
booked by us unless the customer shows equivalent cover.

## Context

A USD 80,000 electronics shipment under ICC (C) was damaged by water; the claim was rejected
because water damage isn't a named peril under (C).

## Outcome

The first claim under the new rule, water damage on AWB-7782, was paid in full.
