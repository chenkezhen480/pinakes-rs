# Getting shipments released from customs holds

## Steps that work

1. Get the hold reason from the broker: document check, physical inspection or valuation query.
2. Compare the declared HS code and value with the commercial invoice and packing list. A
   mismatch in either explains most holds.
3. Send a corrected commercial invoice and packing list, signed by the shipper, within 24 hours.
4. For physical inspections, book the inspection slot the same day; the port charges storage from
   day 3.
5. Tell the consignee the expected release date only after the broker confirms.

## Pitfalls

- Declaring "parts" or "samples" without a specific description triggers valuation queries.
  Seen 7 times.
- Waiting for the shipper to re-sign documents over a weekend adds storage costs. Seen 4 times.
