# Handling failed last-mile deliveries

## Steps that work

1. Read the carrier's exception code: address not found, business closed, or recipient refused.
2. Contact the consignee within 48 hours to confirm the address, a phone number and delivery
   hours.
3. Book a redelivery or a pickup point; business addresses often need a dock appointment.
4. If the consignee refuses, get written instructions from the shipper before the carrier's
   return-to-sender deadline.

## Pitfalls

- Missing the 48-hour window: the carrier returns the parcel and charges return freight.
  Seen 6 times.
- Suite or floor numbers dropped by the shipper's system. Seen 4 times.
