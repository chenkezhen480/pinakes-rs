# Filing damaged cargo claims

## Steps that work

1. Tell the consignee to write the damage on the delivery receipt before signing ("3 cartons
   crushed") — a clean signature weakens the claim.
2. Photos of the outer packaging, the pallet and the damaged goods, before unpacking further.
3. Send a written notice of claim to the carrier and the insurer within 7 days of delivery.
4. For claims over USD 5,000, ask the insurer to appoint a surveyor.
5. Collect the commercial invoice, packing list and repair or replacement quote.

## Pitfalls

- Consignee signed without remarks; the carrier rejected liability. Seen 5 times.
- Notice sent on day 9; the insurer declined. Seen 2 times.
