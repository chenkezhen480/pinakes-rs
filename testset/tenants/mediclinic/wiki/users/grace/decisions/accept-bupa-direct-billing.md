# Accept Bupa direct billing in pediatrics

## Decision

Pediatrics accepts Bupa Global direct billing for outpatient visits.

## Context

Many expat families are insured with Bupa and were choosing other clinics to avoid paying first
and claiming back.

## Outcome

Pediatric visits from Bupa-insured patients doubled in the following month.
