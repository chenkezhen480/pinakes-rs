# Online follow-ups for stable asthma patients

## Decision

Children with stable asthma diagnosed at the clinic can alternate in-person and online follow-up
visits.

## Context

Families travelled up to an hour for short routine follow-ups. The telemedicine rules allow
online follow-ups for conditions already diagnosed in person.

## Outcome

In-person follow-ups dropped by a third, with no increase in urgent visits.
