# Registering international patients

## Steps that work

1. Register with the passport number; the national ID field stays empty.
2. Email the English intake and consent forms when the appointment is booked.
3. Ask for the insurance card and check whether the insurer is on the direct billing list; if
   it isn't, explain self-pay and claim-back before the visit.
4. Book an interpreter for languages other than English and Chinese at least one day ahead.

## Pitfalls

- Registering foreigners with a passport number in the national ID field breaks insurance
  submissions. Seen 4 times.
- Assuming "international insurance" means direct billing works. Seen 6 times.
