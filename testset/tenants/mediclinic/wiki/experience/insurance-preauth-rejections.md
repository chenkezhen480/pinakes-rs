# Handling insurance pre-authorization rejections

## Steps that work

1. Read the insurer's rejection reason: missing documents, not covered, or waiting period.
2. Missing documents: attach the doctor's notes and the itemised estimate, and resubmit within
   2 working days.
3. Not covered or waiting period: tell the patient before the visit, and offer self-pay with a
   receipt they can claim back later.
4. Record the insurer's case number on the appointment.

## Pitfalls

- Telling the patient about the rejection at checkout. Seen 5 times; always ends in a complaint.
- Resubmitting without the policy number printed on the patient's card. Seen 3 times.
