# Fee schedule

| Service | Self-pay price |
|---|---|
| General consultation | 300 yuan |
| Specialist consultation | 600 yuan |
| Online follow-up (video) | 200 yuan |
| Basic blood test panel | 280 yuan |
| X-ray (one view) | 350 yuan |

Tests and procedures are charged separately from the consultation. Patients with direct billing
pay only the co-pay defined in their policy. Prices include VAT; receipts are issued the same
day.
