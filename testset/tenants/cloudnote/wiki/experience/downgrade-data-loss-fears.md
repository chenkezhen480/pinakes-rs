# Customers afraid of losing data when downgrading

## Steps that work

1. Start with the answer: nothing is deleted when a workspace downgrades.
2. Explain what changes: members over the new seat limit become guests, storage over the limit
   makes uploads read-only, and version history older than the new plan's window is trimmed.
3. Offer an export of version history before the downgrade if they care about old revisions.
4. Point out they can upgrade again at any time and everything comes back.

## Pitfalls

- Leading with the limits table makes customers think data will be removed. Seen 6 times.
- Forgetting to mention version history trimming, which is the one thing that is permanent.
  Seen 2 times.
