# Plan limits

| | Team | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Members | 20 | 200 | Unlimited |
| Storage | 100 GB | 1 TB | Unlimited |
| Version history | 30 days | 90 days | Unlimited |
| Guests | 5 | 50 | Unlimited |
| SSO and SCIM | – | – | Included |
| Audit log | – | – | Included |

## Downgrades

Downgrading never deletes notes. Members over the new limit become guests, uploads stop when
storage is over the limit, and version history older than the new window is removed. That last
step can't be undone.
