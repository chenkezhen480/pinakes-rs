# SSO as a paid add-on for Business

## Decision

Business customers can buy SSO as an add-on for 4 USD per seat per month. SCIM stays
Enterprise-only.

## Context

Mid-size customers were churning to competitors that include SSO, and the jump to Enterprise
pricing was too large for them.

## Outcome

Three Business customers bought the add-on in the first month; none churned.
