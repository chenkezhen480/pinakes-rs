# Extended Acme's grace period to 30 days

## Decision

Extend the grace period on invoice INV-2026-0912 for Acme Robotics from 14 to 30 days.

## Context

Acme's annual renewal needed a new purchase order and their procurement team takes about three
weeks. Going read-only on day 14 would have blocked 180 people.

## Outcome

Acme paid on day 22 and renewed for two years on the Enterprise plan.
