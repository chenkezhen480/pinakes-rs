# Settlement cycles and reserves

## Cycles

| Merchant | Cycle |
|---|---|
| Domestic | T+1 (business days) |
| Cross-border | T+2 |
| New merchant, first 30 days | T+7 |

Bank holidays in either country push the payout to the next business day.

## Rolling reserve

For merchants with high chargeback rates (above 1% of transactions) we can hold a rolling
reserve: 10% of each settlement, released after 90 days. The reserve covers chargebacks and
refunds the merchant can't pay.
