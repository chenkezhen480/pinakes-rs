# Merchant KYC requirements

## Documents

- Business licence or certificate of incorporation
- ID documents for all directors
- Proof of business address dated within the last 3 months
- Declaration of ultimate beneficial owners (UBO) holding more than 25%, down to natural persons
- Website or app with a visible refund policy and contact details

## Periodic review

| Risk level | Review every |
|---|---|
| Standard | 12 months |
| High (e-bikes, travel, digital goods) | 6 months |

A review can be postponed once, by up to 3 months, with a compliance officer's approval.
