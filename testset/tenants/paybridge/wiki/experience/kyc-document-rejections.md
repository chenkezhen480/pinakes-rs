# Getting merchant KYC documents accepted

## Steps that work

1. Translate the rejection code into plain words for the merchant.
2. Send a checklist: business licence, director ID, proof of address dated within 3 months, and
   a declaration of every beneficial owner above 25%.
3. Ask for colour scans or photos with all four corners visible.
4. For group structures, ask for the ownership chart up to the natural persons.
5. Re-submit everything together; partial uploads restart the review queue.

## Pitfalls

- Utility bills older than 3 months are the top rejection reason. Seen 12 times.
- Merchants list the holding company as the beneficial owner instead of the people behind it.
  Seen 5 times.
