# Rolling reserve on Voltride

## Decision

Hold a 10% rolling reserve on Voltride E-bikes' settlements for 90 days.

## Context

Voltride's chargeback rate reached 1.8% in August, mostly "not received" disputes after a
warehouse move, above the 1% threshold.

## Outcome

The rate fell to 0.7% within two months. The reserve will be reviewed in December.
