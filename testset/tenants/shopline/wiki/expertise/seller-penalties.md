# Seller penalty rules

## Counterfeit goods

A confirmed counterfeit sale leads to:

- a deduction from the seller's deposit equal to three times the order amount, paid to the buyer
  as compensation;
- 12 violation points;
- removal of the listing.

Three confirmed counterfeit cases within 12 months close the store permanently ("three strikes").

## Other violations

| Violation | Points |
|---|---|
| Late shipment (repeated) | 2 |
| Misleading description | 4 |
| Off-platform payment requests | 12 |

A seller with 48 points in a year is suspended for 30 days. First-time minor cases can end with a
written warning at the operations team's discretion.
