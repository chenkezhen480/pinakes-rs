# Membership tiers

Points are earned at 1 point per yuan paid and count over a rolling 12 months.

| Tier | Points needed | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 1,000 | Birthday coupon (10 yuan) |
| Gold | 5,000 | Free shipping on orders over 49 yuan, priority support |
| Platinum | 20,000 | Free shipping on every order, free returns, dedicated agent |

Tiers are recalculated on the first day of each month. Refunded orders remove their points.
