# Handling counterfeit product claims

## Steps that work

1. Ask the buyer for photos of the product, the serial number and the packaging. Electronics
   serial numbers can be checked on the brand's website.
2. Freeze the seller's payout for this order so the money isn't settled while the claim is open.
3. Send the serial number to the brand's authenticity service; answers usually come in 2–3 days.
4. Confirmed fake: refund the buyer in full plus compensation, and open a seller violation.
   Not confirmed: explain the result to the buyer with the brand's reply attached.

## Pitfalls

- Refunding before freezing the payout means the seller already has the money. Seen 3 times.
- Buyers sometimes compare against grey-market packaging; check the region version before
  calling it fake. Seen 4 times.
