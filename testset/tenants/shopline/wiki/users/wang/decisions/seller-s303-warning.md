# Written warning for 棉语服饰 instead of a penalty

## Decision

Give seller 棉语服饰 (s_303) a written warning instead of 12 violation points.

## Context

A buyer claimed a cotton T-shirt was counterfeit. The seller had no prior violations and supplied
factory invoices; the brand's authenticity check was inconclusive.

## Outcome

The buyer was refunded as a goodwill gesture. No further complaints about the seller in the
following month.
