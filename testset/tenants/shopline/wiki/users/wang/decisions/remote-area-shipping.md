# No free shipping to remote areas

## Decision

Free-shipping promotions exclude Xinjiang, Tibet and Qinghai. Orders to these regions pay a
12 yuan surcharge, shown at checkout before payment.

## Context

Shipping to remote areas costs 3–4 times the national average and the promotions were running
at a loss there.

## Outcome

Complaints about "hidden" fees stopped after the surcharge was shown before payment.
