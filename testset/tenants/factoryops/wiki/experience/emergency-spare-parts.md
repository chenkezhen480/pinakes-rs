# Sourcing spare parts in an emergency

## Steps that work

1. Confirm the exact part number from the machine's parts list, not from memory.
2. Check stock at the sister plant and on idle machines of the same model; borrowing a part is
   faster than any delivery.
3. Ask the supplier for a compatible alternative (same dimensions and rating) if the original
   has a long lead time.
4. Only then pay for express delivery, with the purchase approved by the maintenance lead.
5. Log the emergency with downtime hours so safety stock can be reviewed.

## Pitfalls

- Ordering by description ("400 W servo") and receiving a model with a different encoder.
  Seen 3 times.
- Not logging emergencies, so the same part runs out again. Seen 5 times.
