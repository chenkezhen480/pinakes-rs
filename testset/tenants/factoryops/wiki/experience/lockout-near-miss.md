# Lessons from lockout-tagout near misses

## Steps that work

1. After applying locks, press the start button to verify the machine can't move.
2. Bleed stored energy: release air pressure, block springs and raised parts, discharge
   capacitors on drives.
3. Every person working on the machine puts on their own lock; no shared locks.
4. Report every near miss the same shift, even when nobody was hurt.

## Pitfalls

- Electrical isolation done, but the pneumatic clamp still had pressure. Seen 2 times.
- A contractor worked under someone else's lock and the lock was removed at shift change.
  Seen 1 time.
