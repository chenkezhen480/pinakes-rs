# SMED (quick changeover)

## Steps

1. **Observe**: record a full changeover and list every step with its duration.
2. **Separate**: mark each step as internal (needs the machine stopped) or external (can be done
   while it runs).
3. **Convert**: move internal steps to external — preheat dies, stage tools, preload programs.
4. **Streamline**: quick clamps, standard heights, fewer adjustments, parallel work by two people.
5. **Standardize**: a checklist and a target time for every changeover.

Typical results are a 50–75% reduction in changeover time without new equipment.
