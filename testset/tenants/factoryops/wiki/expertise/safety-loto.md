# Lockout-tagout procedure

1. **Notify** operators and the area supervisor.
2. **Shut down** the machine with its normal stop.
3. **Isolate** every energy source: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity, thermal.
4. **Lock and tag**: each worker applies a personal lock and a tag with their name and date.
5. **Release stored energy**: bleed air and hydraulic pressure, block raised parts, discharge
   capacitors.
6. **Verify**: try to start the machine; it must not move.

Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. Contractors follow the same procedure with
their own locks.
