# Vibration analysis

## ISO 10816 zones (medium machines, 15–75 kW, rigid mounting)

| Zone | RMS velocity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | up to 1.4 mm/s | New machine condition |
| B | 1.4–2.8 mm/s | Acceptable for long-term operation |
| C | 2.8–7.1 mm/s | Plan maintenance; above 4.5 needs attention soon |
| D | above 7.1 mm/s | Risk of damage |

## Reading a spectrum

- 1× running speed: imbalance.
- 2× running speed with high axial vibration: misalignment.
- Non-synchronous peaks at bearing defect frequencies: a failing bearing.

Measure at the same points each time and trend the values; a steady rise matters more than one
reading.
