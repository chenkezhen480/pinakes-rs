# OEE target of 78% for the welding line

## Decision

The 2026 OEE target for welding line L2 is 78%, up from 71% in 2025.

## Context

L2 is the bottleneck between stamping and machining; most of its losses were changeovers and
short stops for nozzle cleaning.

## Outcome

L2 reached 75% by the end of September after the nozzle change rule and changeover work.
