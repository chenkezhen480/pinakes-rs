# Dual-source the 400 W servo motors

## Decision

Qualify a second supplier for the 400 W servo motors used on the welding robots.

## Context

Yaskawa's lead time grew to 10 weeks, and a single failed motor stopped ROB-11 for two days while
waiting for an emergency shipment.

## Outcome

The second source passed trials in September and now covers 40% of demand.
